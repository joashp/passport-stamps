# Monuments used on the stamps

Landmark chosen for each stamp. high/medium: checked in the first research pass. supported: a source named it as the country's best known, most iconic or symbolic landmark. partly: sources confirm it as a major landmark or main sight, or frame it as the symbol of the capital or a region rather than the whole country. changed: replaced after checking. not confirmed: searches found no source backing the pick as a national landmark, so it needs review. unverified: not checked yet. Most support comes from travel and tourism sites, not formal rankings. The set is the 193 UN member states plus the Holy See and Palestine.

195 countries. Frame is the shape family (hand-built frames are unique to their country).
