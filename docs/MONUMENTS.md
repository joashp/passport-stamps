# Monuments used on the stamps

Research notes for the stamp artwork. 'chosen' is what the stamp draws. 'cues' are the features that make the landmark recognisable at stamp size. 'alternates' are other strong candidates if you prefer a different pick. Country list: the ten most visited countries by international arrivals (UN Tourism, 2024).

| Country | Landmark | Where | Confidence | Why | Alternates |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| France (FR) | Eiffel Tower | Paris | high | The best known symbol of France, named as such across the sources. | Arc de Triomphe, Mont Saint-Michel |
| Spain (ES) | Sagrada Familia | Barcelona | high | Spain's most visited monument and the standout pick in the travel sources. | Alhambra, Park Guell |
| United States (US) | Statue of Liberty | New York | high | Ranked first in a worldwide recognition survey (98.7 percent could identify it). | Golden Gate Bridge, Mount Rushmore |
| China (CN) | Great Wall | Badaling and Mutianyu, near Beijing | high | Described as the most celebrated symbol of China. | Temple of Heaven, Terracotta Army, Forbidden City |
| Italy (IT) | Leaning Tower of Pisa | Pisa | medium | Used in your reference image. The Colosseum ranks higher in recognition surveys (4th worldwide), so it is the main alternative. | Colosseum, St Peter's Basilica |
| Turkey (TR) | Hagia Sophia | Istanbul | medium | Istanbul is the headline destination. The sources name Istanbul, Pamukkale, Cappadocia and Ephesus, with no single national symbol. | Blue Mosque (six minarets), Cappadocia fairy chimneys, Pamukkale terraces |
| Mexico (MX) | Chichen Itza, El Castillo | Yucatan | high | A New Seven Wonders site. The Teotihuacan pyramids lead one source, with Chichen Itza close behind. | Pyramid of the Sun, Teotihuacan, Angel of Independence |
| United Kingdom (GB) | Elizabeth Tower (Big Ben) | London | medium | The sources gave the weakest evidence for the UK. Big Ben only appeared in a photo caption, so this pick rests on general recognition. | Tower Bridge, Stonehenge, Tower of London |
| Japan (JP) | Torii gate with Mount Fuji | Itsukushima and Fuji | high | Matches your reference image. Mount Fuji appears in the sources as an iconic landmark. | Kinkaku-ji, Himeji Castle, Tokyo Tower |
| Germany (DE) | Brandenburg Gate | Berlin | medium | A symbol of reunified Germany. The travel sources more often list the Berlin Wall, Cologne Cathedral and Neuschwanstein Castle. | Neuschwanstein Castle, Cologne Cathedral, Reichstag dome |

Medium confidence means there is a strong competing landmark. Say the word and the stamp can switch.
