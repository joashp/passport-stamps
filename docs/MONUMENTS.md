# Monuments used on the stamps

Landmark chosen for each stamp. high/medium: checked in the first research pass. supported: a source named it as the country's best known landmark. partly: a source lists it among the main sights. changed: replaced after checking. not confirmed / unverified: chosen from general knowledge and not checked. Many small countries could not be checked because several list sites were blocked by the session's network allowlist. The set is the 193 UN member states plus the Holy See and Palestine.

195 countries. Frame is the shape family (hand-built frames are unique to their country).
